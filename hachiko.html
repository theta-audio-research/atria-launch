A devotional pad synthesizer for chords that linger.

HACHIKŌ is a deeply expressive software instrument built for warmth, memory and emotional harmony. Play a single note, hold a chord or use the Harmony Chord Board to create slowly evolving soundscapes that feel alive beneath your hands.

No endless menus. No complicated modulation maze.

Just an instrument that waits, remembers and stays.

MEET_THE_HEART

Beautiful before the effects.

At the centre of HACHIKŌ is a carefully tuned 16-voice synthesizer designed to sound rich and complete on its own. Its sound begins with a warm analog and harmonic source, then grows through subtle variations in pitch, timbre, movement and stereo placement.

Every voice is slightly different.
Every chord breathes.

THE_HARMONY_CHORD_BOARD

Press a chord. Let it bloom.

HACHIKŌ's two-row chord board turns rich harmony into a direct, tactile performance. The upper row offers a complete diatonic palette. The lower row contains open, emotionally voiced chords inspired by a treasured piano arrangement. Choose your key. Select a voicing colour. Move between harmonies with smooth, common-tone voice leading.

You don't need to know the theory.
You only need to listen.

STAY

Some sounds should not disappear.

Activate STAY and HACHIKŌ will hold the current harmony while you shape the instrument around it. Add another chord, change the atmosphere or simply let the sound remain.

Release STAY when you are ready.
HACHIKŌ will wait.

THE_MEMORY_GARDEN

See what the instrument remembers.

Every harmony leaves a luminous trace inside the Memory Garden. Notes rise as delicate stems of light, surrounded by ripples, particles and slowly fading colour. Playing, remembered and latched chords each have their own visual life.

MEMORY controls how long the Garden remembers. DEVOTION shapes its intensity. BREATH, GLOW, SPACE, FADE and velocity transform its movement, depth and light.

The display is not a decorative visualizer.
It is the emotional history of your performance.

A small instrument with a long memory.

HACHIKŌ was designed as an instrument to form a relationship with, not another synthesizer to endlessly configure. Its controls are few, but each one reaches deeply into the sound. Its chord board encourages performance rather than programming. Its Memory Garden transforms every session into something visible and temporary.

Warm. Faithful. Patient. Always waiting for you to return.
