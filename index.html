Most lo-fi plugins give you one flavour of broken. CATHODE gives you the whole broadcast path, stage by stage, the way a signal actually degrades on its way from a tape machine to a CRT to a cheap converter.

Each module models a different failure, and because they are chained the damage compounds: dropouts feed interference, interference feeds bitcrushing. Every stage has its own MIX, so you dial the exact amount of ruin.
