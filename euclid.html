GEOMETRIC STEREO PROCESSOR

EUCLID

A stereo processor built on one plane. Every frequency bin in the music is a point on that plane: its horizontal position is log-frequency measured against ORIGIN, its vertical position is how wide it already is. One geometric transform moves every point at once.

The transform runs in one pass: shear, then scale, then rotate. Both coordinates come back out as sound. The new height becomes that bin's Side share, so a point's height is its width. The new horizontal position becomes its delay tap, so its place along the frequency axis is its time. Width and echo are not two effects side by side. They are two readings of one point.

The field on screen is not a visualiser sitting over that. It plots the real post-transform coordinates and the real per-bin energy, taken from the live path. It invents no particles and draws no trails. When the field tilts on screen, the field tilted.

ORIGINThe pivot, in Hz. Everything turns around it. THE FIELDRADIUS scales, ANGLE shears, ROTATION turns, and RATIO splits the scale between the two axes, so the field can stretch in width without stretching in frequency, or the reverse. SPANHow many octaves make one unit across the plane, from half an octave to ten. A narrow SPAN puts the whole transform inside a few octaves; a wide one spreads it across the spectrum. SYMMETRYActs only below ORIGIN. At zero the plane is even either side of the pivot; raise it and the low half folds toward the pivot until it mirrors the high half. GRAVITYCurves the width axis rather than moving it. Positive pulls every point toward the centre line, hardest on the ones already near it. Negative lifts them away. DELAY / FEEDBACK / DEPTHThe per-bin spectral delay the horizontal axis steers. DEPTH is how much of it is summed back in.
THE MONO CONDITION No setting of the geometry touches Mid, and the wet/dry blend is linear rather than equal-power, so Mid cancels exactly on fold-down. With DEPTH at zero, the mono sum of the output is exactly the input, at any geometry and any MIX. Raising DEPTH sums the spectral delay into both channels, and those taps carry Mid. The guarantee is written at DEPTH zero because zero is where it is true.
