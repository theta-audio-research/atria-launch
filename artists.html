The people who actually use this stuff, on the films you have already seen.

THETA instruments are built on a dub stage, not in a vacuum. The engineers below work at the top of feature and episodic post, and several of them ship preset banks inside our plugins, built from the settings they reach for on real shows. Where an artist has authored presets, the modules are listed on their card.
