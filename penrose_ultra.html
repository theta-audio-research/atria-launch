IN ONE SIDE. OUT THE OTHER.

The PENROSE ULTRA interface is drawn as a bridge through spacetime. Your signal falls in on the left, is stripped down to what holds up, and comes out on the right.

The picture is the signal path. The left half only ever takes away: resonance, harshness, mud. The right half only ever gives back: weight, air, glue. You always know which side of the plug-in is doing what, and why.

That makes it fast. Most of the time you will set two or three knobs, listen, and move on to the next episode.

THREE KNOBS

INFALL Take out what is in the way

Removes resonance, harshness and mud, then warms what is left. Ring-y rooms, spitty esses and boomy low mids come down without the mix losing its body.

TEMPER · EDGE · BALLAST · HEAT
EMERGENCE Give back what makes it finished

Adds weight and air, then glues the mix together and holds the ceiling. The step that turns a good rough into something you can publish.

CORE · HALO · GRAVITY
DIALOG Bring the voice to the front

Lifts the range where speech is understood and eases the music bed back underneath it. Works on the finished stereo mix, so it works on the file you already have.

BALANCE · TONE · SENSE

THE VOICE, FORWARD. THE MUSIC, STILL THERE.

You have the final mix. The music bed is a touch hot, the host is a touch buried, and the stems are long gone. DIALOG works on the stereo file you already have.

Turn it up and four bands where speech carries its meaning come forward: body at 500 Hz, clarity at 1 and 2 kHz, presence at 4 kHz. Their depths follow the band-importance weights of the speech intelligibility standard (ANSI S3.5), so the help goes where listeners actually catch words. At the same time two bands where a music bed keeps its energy, 90 Hz and 250 Hz, ease back, clearing the low end that buries a voice two octaves above it.

It follows the talking. When someone speaks, DIALOG leans in. When they stop, it lets the music be music.

It changes balance, not loudness. DIALOG measures exactly what it added and takes that much back, so turning the knob moves the voice forward without a level jump fooling your ears. You judge it by what you hear, and it does not drive the limiter any harder.

BALANCEWhich way the knob works. Toward 0 it only eases the music back. Toward 100 it only brings the voice forward. In the middle it does both.
TONEWhich part of the voice gets the help. Lean toward weight and body for thin voices, or toward edge and presence for muffled ones.
SENSEHow readily DIALOG decides someone is talking. Raise it for a quiet guest in a busy bed. Lower it when the music itself keeps waking it up.

HEAR EXACTLY WHAT DIALOG IS DOING

Press DELTA and PENROSE ULTRA plays only the difference DIALOG makes. The bands it is working on light up red on the SPECTRUM view, so you see and hear the change before you commit to it.

If DELTA sounds like voice, you are in the right place. If it starts to sound like the drums or the pads, back DIALOG off or lower SENSE. It is the fastest way to set the knob by ear and the easiest way to trust what it did.

FOUR LIGHTS. ONE REWARD.

When your programme comes out the other side intact, the whole bridge blooms gold. It is the only reward the interface gives, and it is earned.

Every light is measured on the audio, never read from where the knobs sit. A light that read the knobs would only be a picture of the knobs, and you would stop trusting every other meter on the screen.

Four lights rather than a score, because a score invites chasing a number. Four lights tell you which one is dark and which control fixes it. The verdict reads EXITED INTACT, PASSING THROUGH or OVERDRIVEN.

SEE WHAT HAPPENED, THREE WAYS

One click switches the plate between three views of the same measurements. Every cell on screen is drawn from the audio, so the views never disagree.

SPECTRUM

Input on the left, output on the right, frequency running round the ring from 20 Hz at the bottom to 20 kHz at the top. What INFALL removed and what EMERGENCE and DIALOG added, band by band.

HISTORY

Loudness over the timeline. It follows your host’s playhead, holds still when you stop, and clears only when you press RESET.

FLOW

The signal in motion. Input streams into the core on the left and is emitted on the right. What INFALL cut rides in as white specks, limiting piles up against the ring, and DIALOG’s lift leaves in gold.

LOUDNESS YOU CAN DELIVER TO

Input and output loudness for the last 3 seconds and for the whole pass, loudness range, true peak, how much INFALL removed, how hard HORIZON is limiting, and how much of the time someone is talking. Integrated loudness follows your host’s timeline: it keeps measuring while you play, holds its reading when you stop, and starts again only when you ask it to.

PENROSE ULTRA grows out of PENROSE, THETA’s free two-knob mastering processor. It keeps the bridge, the knobs and the honesty rules, and adds DIALOG, DELTA, three views, timeline metering and four skins.
