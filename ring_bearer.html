RING BEARER

RING BEARER multiplies your sound by a carrier tone. Every note you put in comes out as two: one at its frequency minus the carrier, one at its frequency plus it. Bells, metal, radio voices and broken harmony live in the space between them.

It runs at sixteen times oversampling, so the sidebands stay clean, and it can keep your low end out of the effect entirely. The centre of the panel shows the split happening, measured from your own audio.

What a ring modulator does

At full Depth, the wet signal is your input multiplied by the carrier. Put in a 440 Hz sine with the carrier at 110 Hz and you get 330 Hz and 550 Hz, and no 440 at all. Neither new note is a harmonic of the original, which is why ring modulation sounds metallic rather than distorted.

Turn the carrier down below about 20 Hz and the two sidebands sit so close together that you hear them as a tremolo. Turn it up and the sound breaks apart into bells.

Carrier Shape picks a sine, triangle or square carrier. Triangle and square add odd harmonics of the carrier, and each one forks your input again, so the result gets denser. They are built from a limited series of harmonics, not hard-edged waves, so they never alias.

Tune switches the carrier between free Hz, from 0.5 Hz to 4 kHz, and a musical note and octave, from C−1 to B7 at A4 = 440 Hz. A tuned carrier keeps the sidebands in a key.

The controls

Six knobs across the bottom do the main work. Under each one the panel gives it a second name.

  • CARRIER · THE RINGThe carrier frequency, 0.5 Hz to 4 kHz, or a note when Tune is in note mode.
  • DEPTH · THE INFLUENCEFrom your sound unchanged to full ring modulation.
  • DRIVE0 to 24 dB of saturation, with The Forge behind it. The button above it puts the drive before the ring or after it, on the finished wet signal.
  • STEREO · THE DIVIDEUp to 180° between the left and right carriers, so the two sides modulate differently.
  • MIX · THE PASSAGEA latency-compensated blend of dry and wet.
  • OUTPUT · THE RETURN−24 to +6 dB.

Above them: Carrier Shape, Motion, Tone (a low-pass on the wet signal, from 200 Hz to open), Keep Lows and Tune.

Keep Lows

Ring modulating a bass line moves its fundamental to two new places, and the bottom of the mix goes with it. Keep Lows splits the sound before the effect, anywhere up to 1 kHz, and sends only the top through the ring, the drive and Tone. The lows pass straight through and rejoin afterwards.

The split is a gentle complementary crossover, so with the effect dry the two halves add back up to the original. Off is the default.

Drive and The Forge

Drive saturates the signal at sixteen times oversampling before it reaches the ring. Switch it to POST and it acts on the finished wet sound instead, after the ring and Tone, at the session's sample rate.

Click ADVANCED under Drive, or right-click the knob, to open The Forge: the full saturation stage behind that one knob.

  • CURVE · CASCADESoft, round or smooth curves, in one to four stages.
  • ASYMMETRY · HEADROOMBias for even harmonics, and ±6 dB around the knee.
  • SAGLoud passages lower the rails by up to 15%, so the drive gives under pressure.
  • TRANSIENT PRESERVELets attacks through cleaner than the body of the note.
  • HF ROUNDING · BLENDSoftens the top edge, and blends clean with driven at the same Drive setting.
Motion and texture

The Motion knob moves the carrier up and down by up to 24 semitones, free from 0.02 to 12 Hz or synced from four bars to a sixteenth. The sidebands slide with it.

Click MOTION / TEXTURE to break the wet sound up further: overlapping grains, a patterned stutter locked to your host's tempo, and a pitch warp of up to ±12 semitones. All of it starts at zero, where it changes nothing.

  • GRAINAmount, 10 to 150 ms grain size, and Scatter over the last 100 ms.
  • STUTTERStraight, Pairs, Broken or Gallop patterns in 1/8, 1/16, 1/32 or 1/16 triplet steps, with a Gate for each step.
  • PITCH WARPDrifts the audio itself, separately from the carrier, at the Motion rate.
Seeing the split

The centre display has three views. The Fork finds up to eight of the strongest notes in your input and draws each one splitting at the live carrier: teal for what went in, ember for the note minus the carrier, gold for the note plus it. With a triangle or square carrier the extra forks appear too. The clean lows from Keep Lows run straight through.

Light and Curtain turn the output spectrum into coloured light, where colour is pitch and brightness is energy. Curtain lets the last few seconds fall down the screen, with the carrier drawn as a white line.

Every view is measured from the audio, with a spectrum strip underneath on a 20 Hz to 20 kHz scale. The sideband guide names the strongest input note and the two frequencies the carrier is turning it into.

Input and output meters sit either side, with peak, RMS and clip warnings. Freeze holds the picture while the meters stay live.

Passages

Presets are called passages. The factory set includes CANINERY, TERMINAL YOYO and The Long Walk, and the first artist passages, starting with ilosit's PEW PEW PEW PEW, each under the artist's own heading.

Save your own from the passage menu. Every control is captured, including The Forge and Motion / Texture. A star marks a passage you've changed, and a changed factory passage saves as a copy, never over the original.

SOUND DESIGNERS

Robot voices, bells, transmissions and metal from any source, with a tuned carrier when it needs to sit in a key.

PRODUCERS

Keep Lows holds the bass and kick while the top end rings. Motion and stutter lock to the bar.

GUITAR & SYNTH

Slow carriers for tremolo and movement, fast ones for clang, and The Forge for grit before or after the ring.
