Neighboring moments. Different identities.
An intentional difference library.

DIFFERENTIAL divides sound into tiny fragments and gives each moment its own treatment. Spectral removal, distortion, pitch contours, ring modulation and digital erosion move through an eight-step pattern, turning continuous audio into a shifting sequence of textures.

Keep the changes subtle and let movement emerge inside the sound. Push them further to reshape transients, fold waveforms and create dense rhythmic detail. Every fragment stays within its own boundaries, so the transformation changes the sound without extending it with echoes or feedback tails.

Four effect slots. Eight pattern steps. A different relationship between every moment.

BUILD A PATTERN INSIDE YOUR SOUND

Choose an effect for each of four color-coded slots, then let the eight-step pattern determine their order.

Repeat an effect across several slots with different amounts. Alternate contrasting treatments. Place a soft spectral change beside a sharp burst of distortion, or move between pitch contours and metallic modulation.

Each slot has its own amount control. The global DIFFERENCE control adjusts the contrast between the treatments, while INTENSITY expands their character. Together, they let you move from a connected texture to sharply defined changes without rebuilding the pattern.

TEN WAYS TO CHANGE A FRAGMENT

VOID

Remove parts of the spectrum to change a fragment’s weight and tone. Create hollow passages, narrow textures and gaps in the sound’s frequency content.

FRACTURE

Reshape transients and introduce distortion. Bring out grain and attack, or push the fragment toward a more broken, aggressive texture.

SHIFT

Bend pitch through the fragment with a changing playback contour. Add local pitch movement that can feel elastic, unstable or tightly compressed.

RING

Introduce metallic tones and new frequency relationships through ring modulation. Move from subtle coloration to dense, dissonant detail.

CRUSH

Reduce digital resolution and hold samples to create stepped, eroded textures. Add grit, rough edges and increasingly coarse movement.

VOWEL

Move through vowel-like filter shapes to give audio a changing vocal character. Longer fragments allow the shapes to develop; shorter fragments turn them into brief tonal gestures.

SCATTER

Rearrange smaller pieces inside each fragment. Disrupt its internal order while keeping the result inside the original fragment’s duration.

BEND

Curve the fragment’s playback path so its pitch and timing lean in one direction before settling.

PHASE

Rotate the phase of the fragment against its neighbours to hollow, widen or smear its tone.

PINCH

Squeeze the fragment’s shape toward its centre, tightening its attack and narrowing its body.

Any effect can occupy any slot. Use four different treatments, repeat one across the entire pattern, or explore combinations between those extremes.

THREE WAYS TO CONTROL LENGTH

Fragment length changes how DIFFERENTIAL feels. Longer fragments give each treatment more time to speak. Tiny fragments pack the changes together, creating dense textures that can feel closer to a new timbre than a conventional sequence.

Choose sizes from 8 to 1,024 samples, with three distinct ways to use them.

01FIXED LENGTH

Every fragment uses the same selected size. A consistent grid for precise, repeating transformations.

02RANDOM FIXED

Each of the eight pattern steps receives its own randomly chosen length, then keeps that length on every pass. Build an uneven pattern that remains repeatable. Change the seed to generate another arrangement.

03RANDOM EVOLVING

Choose a new random length every time a step runs. The pattern keeps its order while the time spent on each step continues to change.

In both random modes, MAX SAMPLES sets the upper limit. The fragment boxes show their sample lengths, making the timing visible alongside the effect sequence.

WARP THE INSIDE

DIFFERENTIAL’s timing controls work within the fragments themselves.

SWING introduces elastic timing movement. TRAVEL changes how audio is traversed, including movement toward reverse playback. WARP bends the playback path with opposing PULL and PUSH shapes, while EVOLVE varies that movement over time.

FOLD adds stronger local repetitions and reversals. CYCLE sets the speed of the time oscillator, letting the movement develop slowly or change rapidly.

These controls reshape the audio inside each fragment while preserving its duration. A transformed fragment remains as long as the fragment it came from.

CONTROL THE TRANSITIONS

EDGE controls how abruptly each treatment meets the boundaries of its fragment. Move toward SMOOTH for gentler transitions or toward SEVER for a more exposed, sharply divided result.

Use DIFFERENCE to control the contrast between treatments, then set MIX to blend the processed sound with the original. A separate output control helps balance the result.

The same pattern can become a quiet layer of movement, a rough rhythmic texture or an extreme transformation, depending on how these controls interact.

DROP IN A SOUND

Drag an audio file into DIFFERENTIAL or use LOAD to choose one.

Set the playback position, adjust the START and END points, and loop a region while you explore the effects. The waveform keeps the source visible as you work.

Switch between FILE and LIVE sources to use the loaded audio or process incoming sound. The standalone application provides a place to experiment with files, while the VST3 fits into a host-based workflow.

RANDOMIZE THEN REFINE

RANDOMIZE changes the sound settings, including effect choices, amounts, pattern, timing and length mode, while leaving MIX and OUTPUT untouched.

Use it to find combinations you would not normally build by hand. Keep the parts that work, adjust the rest, or press UNDO to restore the settings from immediately before the latest randomization.

The saved seed makes randomized behavior repeatable. RANDOMIZE and UNDO leave your loaded file, playhead and loop region alone.

SAVE THE SOUNDS YOU FIND

Organize sounds with a categorized preset menu and move through the selected category with the previous and next buttons.

SAVE updates the current user preset. SAVE AS creates a separate sound with its own name and category. An edited indicator shows when the current settings differ from the selected preset.

Presets include the effect and timing controls, length mode, seed, MIX and OUTPUT. They leave the loaded audio and playback region in place, so you can compare different treatments against the same source.

MADE FOR CLOSE LISTENING

Use DIFFERENTIAL to uncover new textures in drum loops, add unstable detail to sustained tones, introduce metallic movement into percussion, or pull unexpected articulation from a voice.

Small changes can alter the relationship between neighboring moments. Extreme settings can turn that relationship into the defining character of the sound.
