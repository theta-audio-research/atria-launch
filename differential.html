DIFFERENTIAL

DIFFERENTIAL cuts your sound into fragments, from 1,024 samples down to 32, and gives each one its own treatment from a library of ten. An eight-step pattern decides which treatment lands where, so neighbouring moments of the same sound come out different from each other.

On the front there are three rings and a ROLL button. Under the hood there is everything else. Press ROLL until you hear it, then shape it with the rings.

How it works

Most glitch effects treat a sound as one long stream and do one thing to it. DIFFERENTIAL treats it as a row of short fragments. Each fragment gets one treatment, at its own amount, and the next fragment gets another.

The treatment stays inside the fragment. A fragment that is pitched, crushed or scattered still lasts exactly as long as the fragment it came from, with no echo or feedback tail spilling into the next one. That is why the result reads as a texture or a rhythm rather than a wash.

The rings push the whole engine at once: CUT for size and repeats, BEND for timing and pitch, DIRT for grit and colour. ROLL deals a new take. MOTION lanes move the rings in time with your song, and HIT restarts things on a drum. After the engine, FUTZ puts the sound in a channel, STRETCH slows it down, and LOW KEEP keeps your low end out of all of it.

Every edge is faded. The rule the engine is built to is glitchy but clean: the cuts are meant to be heard, the clicks are not.

  1. 1SPLIT

    With LOW KEEP on, everything under the crossover steps around the engine untouched.

  2. 2CUT

    The rest is cut into fragments. CHOP sets how small, from 1,024 samples down to 64.

  3. 3TREAT

    The pattern hands each fragment to one of eight slots, and each slot holds one of ten treatments.

  4. 4PLACE

    FINAL PITCH transposes the result, FUTZ puts it in a channel, STRETCH slows it down.

  5. 5REJOIN

    The kept lows come back in, lined up, and a soft ceiling from −1 dBFS keeps the output under full scale.

The three rings

Each ring carries two controls: one on the inner circle, one on the outer. Turn right for more glitch. The rings never overwrite what is underneath them. They push a copy of the settings on the way into the engine, so with every ring at zero the plug-in sounds exactly like the settings under the hood, and a preset always comes back the way you saved it.

CUT

CHOP · REPEAT

CHOP makes the fragments smaller, down to 64 samples, and raises the contrast between neighbours as it goes. Smaller fragments get softer edges, never sharper ones. REPEAT folds each fragment back on itself into local repeats and reversals; on a loaded file it walks a stutter ladder from 8 and 16 pulses up through tresillo to 32 and 64 rolls.

BEND

MOTION · PITCH

MOTION loosens time inside the fragments: swing, travel, warp and drift all rise together. PITCH is centred. Turn it either way to transpose the finished sound and bend the slots that shift pitch, up or down to two octaves.

DIRT

CRUSH · COLOR

CRUSH raises the intensity and every slot's amount at once. COLOR is centred too: left warms the sound with saturation, right brightens it with harder edges and more bite.

Grab a ring anywhere: the centre disc moves the inner control, and everything else on the ring moves the outer one. Each ring has a HOLD button, which keeps ROLL's hands off it, and a LANE button, which opens its MOTION lane.

ROLL and WILD

ROLL deals a new take: new treatments in the slots, a new pattern and seed, and new positions for every ring that is not held. Every take is kept. The arrows either side of ROLL walk back through them, and an earlier take comes back exactly, so you can roll ten times and keep the third.

WILD, under ROLL, sets how far it goes. At low settings ROLL stays near what you have. At the default of 80% it reaches the full range of the rings. Pitch follows WILD too: wilder takes transpose more often and further, by plain intervals, up to two octaves, but never more than an octave down. ROLL never adds STEPS or a sliding glide, so a take cannot pulse the bass of a loop in and out like a tremolo. STEPS is still there for you to choose yourself. It always returns PITCH to centre and builds from there.

ROLL leaves your setup alone. FUTZ, STRETCH, LOW KEEP, the MOTION lanes, HIT, MIX and WILD itself belong to you: ROLL, RANDOMIZE and UNDO never change them. Your loaded file, playhead and loop region stay where they are too.

Under the hood there is also RANDOMIZE, which reaches every sound setting rather than the rings, with a one-step UNDO.

Ten treatments

Any treatment can sit in any of the eight slots, each slot at its own amount. Use eight different ones, the same one eight times at different strengths, or anything between.

  • VOIDTakes parts of the spectrum away, for hollow, narrow passages and gaps in the tone.
  • FRACTUREReshapes the transient and drives the fragment into distortion, from grain to broken.
  • SHIFTBends pitch through the fragment with a changing playback contour: elastic, unstable or tight.
  • RINGRing modulation, from a faint metallic colour to dense, dissonant detail.
  • CRUSHLowers the resolution and holds samples, for stepped, eroded texture.
  • VOWELMoves through vowel-like filter shapes. Long fragments speak; short ones become tonal flicks.
  • SCATTERShuffles smaller pieces inside the fragment, and keeps the result inside its length.
  • BENDCurves the playback path so pitch and timing lean one way before they settle.
  • PHASERotates the fragment's phase against its neighbours to hollow, widen or smear the tone.
  • PINCHSqueezes the fragment toward its centre, tightening the attack and narrowing the body.
Motion lanes and HIT

Each ring has a lane: a modulator locked to your host's tempo and bar position. Pick a shape (sine, triangle, saw up, saw down, square, sample-and-hold, or eight drawn STEPS), a rate from a thirty-second triplet to four bars, a depth either way, and a smoothing amount. Send it to the ring's inner control, its outer one, or both.

A lane adds to the ring; it never moves it. The ring stays where you set it, the lane rides on top, and at depth zero it adds exactly nothing. Lanes are off by default.

HIT listens for drum hits and restarts the step pattern, the lanes, or both, so the glitch lands on the beat instead of drifting across it. It listens to the input, or to a sidechain when your host connects one. SENSITIVITY sets what counts as a hit, and HOLD-OFF, 20 ms to 1 s, sets how soon it can fire again.

FUTZ and Dialog FX

FUTZ runs after the engine, on the finished sound, and does what a radio, a phone or a small speaker does to a voice: a steep band-limit, overload, and a bed of noise (hiss, static, or hum and whine), with a gate that lets the noise breathe with the voice. The engine makes the sound move. FUTZ makes it sit in a channel you believe.

It has its own ON switch and is off by default. Every change is ramped, so it never adds a click the audio did not already have.

Sixteen Dialog FX presets put the two together for post and sound design:

Stretch

STRETCH sits at the very end of the chain and plays the most recent audio back slower, up to sixteen times. After WINDOW seconds it jumps back to live with a crossfade and starts again, so the slowed picture never drifts far from the music. Short windows give glitchy jumps; long ones give a frozen wash.

SMEAR runs from clear to blurred. At zero the phases stay locked around the peaks, so transients and tones hold together; turned up, it melts into a pad. MIX blends it with the sound underneath, lined up.

Off, STRETCH is an exact bypass. On, it adds one frame of latency, about 21 ms, and reports it to your host only while it is on.

Low keep

Glitching a bass line or a kick usually costs you the floor of the mix. LOW KEEP splits the sound before the engine with a fourth-order Linkwitz-Riley crossover, set anywhere from 60 to 300 Hz. Everything below passes untouched. Only the top goes into the engine, and through FUTZ and STRETCH after it.

The kept lows wait in a delay line and rejoin after STRETCH, lined up with whatever latency the chain has at that moment. With the engine dry, the two halves sum flat. OFF is the default, and an exact bypass.

Under the hood

UNDER THE HOOD opens the whole engine. Each of the eight slots has its treatment and amount. The pattern sets their order. Fragment length can be fixed, fixed per step at random, or new at random every time a step runs. SWING, TRAVEL, WARP, EVOLVE, FOLD and CYCLE move time inside the fragments. EDGE runs from SMOOTH to SEVER, and DIFFERENCE sets the contrast between neighbours.

Two rhythm modes lay a pattern over the whole sound. STUTTER repeats captured audio; BUCEPHALUS puts bouncing gaps into audio that keeps moving. Sixteen patterns, from 8 PULSE and 64 ROLL to HEARTBEAT, REVERSE and PITCH FALL, fitted to the selection or locked to tempo.

CHARACTER sets the engine's swarm: SWARM, NEON DREAM, DUBSTEP, LIQUID DNB, INDUSTRIAL, DREAMSCAPE or TAPE DRIFT, each one steering how the slots drift and mutate.

Drop a file onto the panel to work on it directly. Set START and END, loop the region, play it through the effect with DRY to compare, and EXPORT what you hear. Switch SOURCE to LIVE to go back to the track.

The licence

The trial is the whole plug-in for seven days, and the header counts down the days left. After that, a computer that has not been activated goes silent, and the LICENCE card stays over the panel until a licence is activated. In GarageBand and other sandboxed hosts it keeps playing.

The same installer everyone buys, running unlicensed for seven days. A licence unlocks it in place, no reinstall.

Activation needs an internet connection once. After that DIFFERENTIAL runs offline, and the licence does not expire. One licence covers three computers, and one activation covers every user account on that Mac. Release a computer from the LICENCE card to move its seat, without writing to us.

Once activated, DIFFERENTIAL is never silenced by a licence check. After a hardware change or a system reinstall it keeps playing in full, and asks you to enter the same key again.

Who it is for
PRODUCERS & BEATMAKERS

Roll a drum loop until it breaks the right way, keep the take, and lock it to the bar with HIT and a lane. LOW KEEP leaves the kick where it was.

SOUND DESIGNERS

Ten treatments, eight slots and fragments down to 32 samples, where a pattern stops sounding like a sequence and starts sounding like a new timbre. STRETCH turns any of it into a frozen bed.

POST & DIALOG

Sixteen Dialog FX for radios, phones, scanners and bad video calls, with the engine there for the breakups and dropouts a static filter cannot do.

ARTISTS & EXPERIMENTERS

Push WILD up and ROLL for surprises. Every take is kept, so nothing you liked is ever one click from lost.
