01 Enter your email

02 Open the link

LICENCE_HELP

I cannot find my key

It is in your order receipt email and in every order after you sign in. If nothing shows up, you probably checked out with a different address. Try the other one.

New computer

Your key moves with you. If the new machine will not activate, contact support with your order email and we will reset your activations.

Trials and free plugins

They appear in your orders too, with their installers. A trial has no key: buy the plugin and the key it comes with unlocks the copy you already installed.
