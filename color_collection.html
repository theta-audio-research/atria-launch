MOIRÉFlanger #397A78 · v0.1.4

A comb-surface flanger with true thru-zero sweep: sine, triangle, or random motion, bipolar feedback, and a compressor on the tail to hold the resonance.

CONCORDChorus #9A7B45 · v0.1.0

Chorus as a voice braid: two to eight voices with independent spread and detune, and a BBD mode for the dark bucket-brigade lane.

GIREPhaser #C6537C · v0.2.0

An allpass-surface phaser voiced three ways, JFET, OPTICAL, or DUAL, with stages to 2×6 and an ultra-slow ×20 cycle.

CINDERDistortion & Saturation #B85D52 · v0.0.1

Distortion and saturation in three circuits, EMBER, CHAR, ASH, with bias and sag up front and a tube-and-iron stage behind the drive.

VESTIGEReverb #536F9D · v0.1.0

A reverb built on three engines, DENSE, MOD, and PLATE, drawn as time × band × energy while you shape the tail.

EUCLIDShifted Delay #7556A8 · v0.0.11

A shifted delay on a live field: radius and origin set the shift, angle and rotation steer the taps, and the delay field below sets time, feedback, and depth.
