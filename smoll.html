SMOLL

SMOLL is a small-room reverb for film and TV: bathrooms, bedrooms, closets, kitchens, hallways, cars and stairwells. The rooms dialogue actually gets recorded in, so ADR and added lines sit where the scene was shot.

It runs in mono, stereo, LCR, quad, 5.0 and 5.1. On a surround aux fed by a mono send, the reverb follows the send's panning.

Place the source

The room map on the right of the panel is a plan of the room with the mic at the centre. Drag the source around it, and pull it back from the mic to move a line from close up to across the room.

Put SMOLL on a surround aux fed by a mono send and turn on Follow Pan: the reverb follows wherever the send is panned. Follow Amount, Centre, Rear and LFE set how much goes where.

Along the bottom: Low Cut, High Cut, Early / Late, Movement and Output. Early / Late balances the reflections against the tail; Movement adds a slow, random drift so a long line never sounds frozen.

A and B compare two settings, Undo steps back, and Bypass never replays an old tail when you switch back in.

Why it doesn't ring
  • EARLY REFLECTIONSAn image-source model of the actual room, with the mic at the centre and the source where you put it. Each reflection is jittered slightly, because real walls aren't mirrors, and smeared into a short burst that differs per speaker, so tiny rooms don't comb-filter.
  • THE TAILA 16-line feedback delay network in which every line feeds every other, with prime delay lengths, an allpass inside each loop and three-band decay where the highs always die first.
  • MEASUREDTuned against real room recordings. The late tail is as rough as the real rooms and as rough as ideal random noise, which is what keeps a small room from sounding metallic.
