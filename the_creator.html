Creator of Worlds

The world is full of instruments.

Drop in any sound. See it as light. Grab the pieces you love with a magic wand and play them from your keyboard. THE CREATOR turns field recordings, vocals, machines, and accidents into playable instruments, in seconds, not sessions.

Every recording is a stack of layers: a fundamental here, a shimmer of harmonics there, a breath of noise across the top. THE CREATOR shows you all of it as a living spectral display, then hands you a wand.

Click a harmonic and it finds the whole partial. Drag across a texture and it gathers the energy under your stroke. Whatever you select becomes the instrument: full fidelity, key-tracked, polyphonic, immediately playable.

No layers to configure. No science-project workflow. One canvas, one wand, and your ears.

WHAT_IT_DOES

[01]

The wand

Click any point of light and the wand snaps to the strongest partial under your cursor, then flood-fills the connected energy. SIZE sets its reach, a sliver of one harmonic or a whole gesture. Drag to paint, alt-drag to erase, INVERT for everything-except. SOFT feathers the selection edges from surgical to melted, or all the way down to a hard, ringing vintage mask. Zoom to 100× for the fine work.

[02]

Real spectral playback

Your selection isn't a filter approximation. THE CREATOR re-renders the audio through a full spectral pass, what you selected is exactly what plays, at full fidelity, with soft mask edges that never ring. Selections survive saving, reloading, and your DAW's undo habits.

[03]

Speed, pitch and tone, actually independent

SPEED stretches from quarter-speed to 4× without touching pitch. PITCH transposes two octaves either way without touching duration. TONE strips a sound down to its harmonic skeleton or blooms it open into air. Three knobs, no chipmunks.

[04]

Play it like you found it, or shuffle the deck

Grab five moments from a recording and choose how they answer your keys: everything at once, one selection per note in order, or a dice roll per note. Flip on SUSTAIN and any selection holds forever, LOOP crossfades, BOUNCE ping-pongs back and forth with no seam at all. Two seconds of found sound becomes an endless pad.

[05]

A spectral view worth staring at

The display is rendered on the GPU: light sweeps drift across the water, bright energy blooms, playheads trail like comets and throw star-glints as they cross loud moments. Six view themes, Inferno, Glacier, Viridis, Gold, Ultra, Silver, each with its own shimmer and playhead colour. It's the prettiest way anyone has ever looked for a sound.

[06]

Honest engineering

The resynthesis engine ships with a test suite: bit-perfect reconstruction with a full selection, −170 dB rejection outside it, pitch shifts that land exactly on frequency at exactly the same length. If a control is on the panel, it does what it says.
