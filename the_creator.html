THE CREATOR

THE CREATOR turns any recording into an instrument you play from the keyboard. Drop in a sound and it appears as a field of light. Touch the parts you want with the wand, and those parts become the instrument: key-tracked, polyphonic, at full fidelity.

Load four sounds at once and every key plays all four. Blend them with a ball you can throw, rebuild them from their partials, slow the whole machine to a crawl, and print the effects as an impulse response.

The field of light

The loaded sound drawn as marks of light, with the wand selection lit. Click to find a partial, drag to paint, alt-drag to erase. The four layer slots, A to D, sit beside it.

The whole window is one pane of glass. The knob deck, PERFORM and the keyboard stay on screen; the six tabs between them open each part of the instrument, and none of the pages scroll.

The wand

Every recording is a stack of layers: a fundamental here, a shimmer of harmonics there, a breath of noise across the top. THE CREATOR draws all of it as a field of light, one mark for every point of energy, so small changes in the sound show.

Click a harmonic and the wand finds the whole partial under your cursor and follows its connected energy. SIZE sets its reach, from a sliver of one harmonic to a whole gesture. Drag to paint, alt-drag to erase, or draw a lasso round a region.

INVERT keeps everything except what you touched. SOFT feathers the edge of a selection from surgical to melted. Zoom in for the fine work.

Whatever you select is what plays. Imports WAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3, M4A and OGG, and a saved patch carries its own audio, so you share an instrument, not a file hunt.

Four sounds, one key

Load a sound into each of the four layers, A to D, and every key you press plays them together. Each layer keeps its own selection, gain, pan and tune, and a strip of the four files runs along the top of every page.

Choose how a recording answers your keys: ALL plays every selection at once, STEP plays one selection per note in order, RANDOM rolls the dice per note.

SUSTAIN holds a selection for as long as you hold the key: LOOP crossfades, BOUNCE ping-pongs with no seam, FREEZE hangs one instant in the air, GRAIN turns it to weather. Two seconds of found sound becomes an endless pad.

HARMONY stacks notes on every key: an octave up or down, a fifth, an octave and fifth, or the three pad stacks OCTAVES, WIDE and SUB, detuned a few cents apart so they beat gently like a unison.

Three engines
ELASTIC

Speed and pitch, apart

Stretch a note from quarter speed to four times while it sounds, and transpose two octaves either way without changing its length. Grab the knob mid-chord and the sound follows.

RESAMPLE

The tape-style coupling

Pitch and speed move together, like a sampler or a varispeed deck. Higher notes play faster and brighter, lower ones slower and darker.

PARTIAL

Rebuilt from its partials

The sound is taken apart into hundreds of moving sine partials and played back as an additive synth, so you can reshape it in ways a sampler cannot. It has a page of its own.

PARTIAL resynthesis

Switch the VOICE page's ENGINE row to PARTIAL and THE CREATOR analyses the layer into its partials: up to 1,024 in the middle of the image and 512 at the sides, so a stereo sound keeps its width. MODE chooses which ones a smaller count keeps: the loudest, the longest-lived, the harmonics of the note, or one from every band.

The PARTIAL page shows every partial as a dot while you play, with the source and the played note drawn as outlines over them, and a count of how many are sounding.

  • PHASEAcross the pad. How the partials line up: as recorded, all on one clock for the sharpest pulse, or spread out for the smoothest wash.
  • CEPSTRUMUp the pad. Smooths each partial's level towards the overall shape of the sound, from detailed to a soft outline.
  • TRANSPOSE · SHIFTMove the partials up to four octaves, and move the formant (the vowel of the sound) on its own.
  • LOCOPulls every partial onto the harmonics of the note you play, so noise and inharmonic sounds start to sing in key.
  • QUANT · LATCHHolds the partials still and moves them on a grid, from 1/64 to two bars, synced to the host.
  • WAVEPlays every partial on sine, triangle, saw, square, stepped or pulse, or on a wave you load yourself.
The morph ball

The MORPH page is the pad alone. Engage QUAD and the ball becomes a vector mixer: each corner solos a layer, the centre blends all four. Drag it, orbit it, or throw it, and it bounces off the walls with real momentum while the mix rides along.

Or load four complete sounds into the corners and glide between them. RND scatters each parameter's path so no two journeys match, and ORBIT sets the ball circling on its own.

MOVE X and MOVE Y put LFO 1, LFO 2 or the mod wheel on each axis of the pad, with their own depth, and the blend plays itself.

Motion

The MOTION page holds two LFOs with five shapes, including a slow Drift, free or synced to the host.

They and the mod wheel reach thirteen targets: filter, echo, space and level; pitch, speed, grain and both axes of the morph ball; and PARTIAL's shift, transpose, phase and cepstrum.

Effects and SLO-MO

The FX page runs the whole chain inline: drive (soft, fold or crush), chorus, a tempo-synced echo with ping-pong, and a space with FREEZE. The filter, grain and send levels stay on the knob deck below. Flip ROUTING to SEND and each layer feeds the four effects on its own, so one layer can drown in echo while its neighbour stays dry.

EXPORT IR prints the rack as it stands to a WAV impulse response, ready to load in any convolver.

  • SLO-MO · DILATEPull the throttle and everything slows together: the notes, the echoes, the LFOs. Past ×8 the echoes regenerate and a crystal engine shatters the sound into reversed shards.
  • SLO-MO · TAPEA tape stop and start with its own curve, on a key you hold.
  • SLO-MO · PADCaptures what you play and smears it into a drifting, blooming pad.
Signal path
  1. 1SOURCE

    Up to four recordings, each with its own wand selection.

  2. 2VOICE

    Elastic, Resample or Partial, up to 32 voices, with harmony and unison.

  3. 3MORPH

    The ball mixes the four layers or glides between four stored sounds.

  4. 4MOTION

    Two LFOs and the mod wheel on thirteen targets.

  5. 5FX

    Drive, chorus, echo and space, in series or per layer, plus filter and grain.

  6. 6SLO-MO

    Dilate, tape stop or pad, then the output level.

Eleven skins

THE CREATOR keeps its own look in eleven skins. Six are the original spectral palettes, INFERNO, GLACIER, VIRIDIS, GOLD, ULTRA and SILVER; five are printed skins that recolour the whole pane, SURVEY, ACID, BLUEPRINT, NIGHT and RING BEARER. The skin is a view setting, so it never changes the sound.

Patches and macros

Factory patches arrive with their own source audio, so every one plays the moment it opens. Each carries four named macros on the PERFORM strip: one knob moves up to eight parameters at once, and the patch keeps its own values underneath.

Right-click a macro to assign it. Step through patches with the arrows in the PATCH bar, and save your own.

Stereo out, MIDI in. Sessions recall every layer, selection and mix; samples are referenced by file, with relink, and a sound with no file behind it is saved inside the session.

Every build runs a test suite before it ships: a full selection must rebuild the recording, a selection must reject what lies outside it, pitch shifts must land on frequency at the same length, and the exported IR must match the rack it came from. If a control is on the panel, it does what it says.
