RETURN_TO_LAB STORE

A two-knob mastering processor. Signal falls into the black hole on the left, is stripped to what survives the throat, and emerges from the white hole on the right. The picture is not a skin over a mastering chain. It is a drawing of one.

THE_BRIDGE

Drag either control. Each knob is a position along the tube, not a brightness. The throat burns harder the closer the two fronts get to it.

THE_TWO_CONTROLS

Infall

Everything left of the throat. Turn it up and more is taken: resonant peaks flattened, harshness pulled back, low mud cleared, and what remains warmed as it passes through.

No setting of Infall adds anything.

Emergence

Everything right of the throat. Weight underneath, air above, glue across the middle, and a ceiling that stays where you put it.

No setting of Emergence removes anything.
