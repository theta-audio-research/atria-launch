A T R I A

ATRIA is a sonic exploration instrument. Twelve modules (stutter, varispeed, pitch, crush, flange, phase, warble, tube drive and more) sit behind six macros on three rings. Turn one ring and a whole group of modules moves together, each in its own measure.

It comes as two plug-ins from one engine: ATRIA, an insert effect for anything you feed it, and ATRIA PLAYER, an eight-voice synth that plays straight into the same chain.

The idea

Most multi-effects give you a row of modules and a knob for everything. ATRIA gives you the knobs, but you rarely need them. Each of the six macros is a musical direction: more motion, more displacement, more pulse, more space, more drive, more colour. Turning one right always means more of that thing.

Behind each macro sits a set of weighted links into the modules. FLOW, for example, moves the stutter timing and the warble's speed and drift strongly, the transient release and the flange and phase rates a little, and opens the stereo width a touch.

The links are yours to change. Right-click any knob to drive it from a ring, or make it follow another knob, from −100% to +100%; a negative depth inverts it. The factory links show in the same menu, with their depths, so you can retune or remove them.

Two link buttons join the rings: INNER chains FLOW, PULSE and DRIVE, OUTER chains SPACE, COLOR and SHIFT. With a link on, one gesture sweeps three rings at once.

Six macros
  • A · FLOWSmooth to chaotic timing. Stutter timing and the warble's wow and drift lead; flange and phase rates follow.
  • B · SHIFTStable to warped playback. Pitch and varispeed displacement, with the stutter grains and the warble moving in sympathy.
  • C · PULSEAttack and rhythm. The transient shaper and both stutters, from soft and breathing to chopped and punchy.
  • D · SPACEWidth and depth. Flange and phase, the stutter's space and the summing amp's stereo spread.
  • E · DRIVEEnergy. The tube drive, crush and the compressor push harder together.
  • F · COLORTone, dark to bright, through the drive's tone and air and the tone EQ's tilt.
Signal path
  1. 1SOURCE

    Your audio, or the PLAYER's eight voices.

  2. 2TIME

    Transient, stutter A, varispeed and pitch.

  3. 3SPLIT

    Crush, flange and phase on one branch, the dry signal on the other.

  4. 4SUM

    The summing amp joins them, then stutter B and warble.

  5. 5ENERGY

    Premium drive, tone EQ and compressor.

  6. 6OUT

    Output gain and a lookahead limiter.

The split is the heart of it: the crush, flange and phase branch and the clean branch are summed with their own blend, width, balance and phase, so the dirt sits beside the original instead of replacing it.

Twelve modules
  • TRANSIENTAttack and release shaping with its own character and gain.
  • STUTTER A · BTwo stutters, before and after the split, with space, time, fade, shape and host sync.
  • PITCH · SPEEDA pitch shifter and a varispeed from a quarter to four times, with clean splices.
  • CRUSHBits, rate, dither, fold and noise, the same sound at every sample rate.
  • FLANGE · PHASEA flanger with feedback, manual and width, and an eight-stage phaser with spread and centre.
  • OSCILLATORTwo morphing oscillators with an envelope: a drone to mix in under ATRIA, the voices of ATRIA PLAYER.
  • WARBLETape wow, flutter, age, drift and noise, oversampled.
  • SUMMING AMPJoins the two branches: blend, width, level, phase rotation, balance, crosstalk and headroom.
  • PREMIUM DRIVEA tube drive with a parallel blend, tone, focus, body and air.
  • COMP · LIMA stereo-linked compressor, then a 1 ms lookahead limiter on the output, after a tilt tone EQ.
ATRIA PLAYER

ATRIA PLAYER is the same engine as an instrument. Eight voices, two oscillators each, render into the head of the chain, so the rings, the stutters, the warble and every link you make process the synth itself.

The oscillator morphs continuously from sine through triangle and saw to square, band-limited at every point. The second oscillator detunes against the first by up to 25 cents.

An ADSR envelope, velocity, pitch bend and the sustain pedal. Octave, wave, detune and the envelope are all link targets: let DRIVE morph the wave, or tie the release to SPACE.

One licence covers both plug-ins.

Presets are patches

An ATRIA preset is not a list of knob positions. It stores the routing: which ring drives which module, how hard, and which knobs follow which. Load one and you load a way of moving, then play it with the six macros.

Modulation is worked out on a fixed grid every 32 samples, so a session sounds the same at any buffer size, and nothing the rings do is ever written back into your automation.

The dry path is always delayed to match, so engaging pitch never shifts your timeline.
