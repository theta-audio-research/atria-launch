COMPLIANCE

COMPLIANCE is a true-peak limiter and a BS.1770 loudness and QC tool in one plug-in, for delivery from stereo up to 9.1.6.

It measures the programme and the dialogue, judges the mix against the delivery spec you choose, and tells you which part of the spectrum is pushing you over. The limiter holds the ceiling on every channel.

ONE MEASUREMENT · EVERY SPEC
Measure

COMPLIANCE measures loudness to ITU-R BS.1770, revisions 1 to 4, gated for the whole programme and gated on dialogue. It reads momentary, short-term and integrated loudness, loudness range, true peak and true peak after downmix.

It also estimates how much of the programme is speech, and when speech drops under 15% or 10%, it switches to the programme measurement the way the delivery specs require.

Measure along the host timeline, so a relocate starts a fresh reading, or continuously while the transport is stopped. Freeze, hold and reset the map at any point.

When you are done, Export QC writes a report from the same measurement the panel is showing.

Judge

Pick a delivery spec in the rail and COMPLIANCE judges every number against it. The QC view judges one measurement against eight specs side by side, with a margin ladder for the one you picked, so you see at once which deliveries pass and by how much.

See why

The 1770 view draws what the meter hears. PANES stacks the raw, live, post-limiter, risk and 1770-weighted spectra as glass panes; STACK shows the measurement chain from input through the pre-filter, RLB weighting, mean square and gating to the integrated value.

WEIGHTING, LAYERS and VIEW show the same signal other ways, and QC holds the spec matrix.

The rail names the band under the most pressure, from RUMBLE, WEIGHT, BODY, CLARITY or AIR, and one button takes you to it on the BANDS page.

A gain-reduction terrain shows what the limiter did to every channel of the live layout, up to 9.1.6.

Limit
  • TRUE-PEAK BRICKWALLA lookahead limiter with BS.1770-4 4x true-peak detection on every channel and oversampling up to 32x. Surround and height channels hold the same ceiling as L and R.
  • LINK MODEPairs, Groups, Global or Multi-Mono, with Front Follow, so a centre-channel line doesn't pump the whole bed.
  • DMX SAFELimits the fold-down too: a 2.0 downmix to BS.775 is checked with its own 1.5 ms lookahead and trimmed before it can go over.
  • MULTIBANDFive bands (RUMBLE, WEIGHT, BODY, CLARITY, AIR), each with its own soft-knee compressor, before the brickwall. Off by default.
  • DELTA · VIEW ONLYHear only what the limiter removes, or measure without touching the audio, delay-matched so the host's compensation stays right.
  • DITHERTPDF dither on the output.
