The circuits
RED036_RED · Saturation / distortion

Distortion with weight

Turn the central control to add harmonic richness, low-mid body and controlled intensity. Subtle settings bring depth and cohesion; push it for thick saturation and rounded peaks.

AUTO LEVEL is on by default: it holds the output at the loudness of the input, so turning RED up changes the sound, not the volume. A smooth peak compressor keeps the punch and a clean clipper catches what is left, with 4× oversampling behind it all.

GREEN037_GRN · Delay / detuned tails

Delay, pulled out of shape

Tap the main knob to set the tempo, or switch on HOST SYNC and follow the session. Then turn it: wet level, feedback, detune, drift, diffusion, width and tone move together along one curve, from a clean echo to a wide field of shifting repeats.

Every pass adds a little opposing pitch movement and a little darkness, so the tail widens and fades like it is going somewhere. Pull TONE down and the echoes start to swim. STEREO PONG bounces it all across the field.

BLUE038_BLU · Modulation / liquid core

Turn sound into motion

One macro coordinates width, phase, delay, feedback and an evolving irregularity, so the movement feels alive rather than looped. Low settings add gentle depth; the top of the range is wide, unstable and still musical.

Three water modes: CLEAR for crisp phase detail, DEEP for wide liquid movement, ABYSS for dark, slow, delay-driven depth.

BROWN039_BRN · Bilateral transient

Shape the hit. Hold the weight.

Turn left to soften attacks and shorten sustain. Turn right to sharpen transients and bring the body forward. Centre is zero processing.

Behind the knob is the transient engine from HORME, moving attack and sustain together from one broadband detector, so the whole sound moves as one with no band splitting. Zero latency, so it sits anywhere.

GREY040_GRY · Compressor

Compression, without the complication

Turn the central control to move from gentle levelling to firm, confident compression. GREY coordinates threshold, ratio, knee, attack, hold and release for you, and its automatic makeup gain restores the body while the peaks stay down.

The pin-matrix display reads the music: a quiet skyline at rest, amber flashes when it is working. Open Advanced for the full compressor.

BLACK041_BLK · Limiter / clipper

Limit. Clip. Control.

One knob moves from controlled peak limiting into assertive clipping, coordinating drive, limiting and clipping as one gesture. Low settings tighten and contain; at maximum pressure it does not pretend to be polite.

The circular labyrinth thickens under pressure and flashes coral when peaks are caught. AUTO LEVEL lets you hear the shape of the limiting, not just more volume.
