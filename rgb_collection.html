RED 036_RED · SATURATION / DISTORTION

Distortion with weight

RED is a one-knob distortion designed to make sounds feel bigger, warmer and more alive. Turn the central control to add harmonic richness, low-mid body and controlled intensity. At subtle settings, RED brings depth and cohesion. Push it further for thick saturation, rounded peaks and unmistakable color.

More body. More energy. No level surprises.

RED automatically balances its output as the distortion increases, helping you make honest decisions without being distracted by volume changes. A smooth peak compressor maintains punch, while the final clean clipper catches excess transients without flattening the sound.

One knob when you want it. Deeper control when you need it.

The main interface keeps RED fast and instinctive. Open the Advanced panel to shape the finer details:

Built for anything that needs weight.

Use RED to add substance to drums, reinforce bass, warm synths, energize guitars, thicken vocals or give an entire mix more density. From barely-there enhancement to heavy velvet saturation, RED stays musical throughout its range.

See the sound come alive.

GREEN 037_GRN · DELAY / DETUNED TAILS

Delay, pulled out of shape

GREEN is a one-knob delay and echo built around detuned, drifting tails. Set the tempo by tapping the main control, then turn it to move from a restrained echo to a wide, diffused field of shifting repeats. Your delay time stays locked — the knob controls the character and intensity around it.

One knob. A whole ecosystem of echoes.

Turn GREEN to increase the wet level, feedback, detune, drift, diffusion, width and tonal depth together. Every part of the sound evolves along one carefully tuned musical curve. At lower settings, repeats remain clean and controlled. Push further and they spread, darken, soften and move increasingly out of tune.

Tap. Turn. Let it grow.

Tap the main knob to set the tempo. GREEN detects and averages your rhythm, then locks the delay to a quarter-note pulse. Changing the knob afterward won’t disturb the timing.

Detuned tails

Every pass through the feedback path accumulates subtle opposing pitch movement. The center stays grounded while the tail becomes wider, less stable and more alive.

Stereo Pong

Activate Stereo Pong to send the repeats alternating across the stereo field. Each bounce continues through GREEN’s detuning, diffusion, tone and drift architecture.

Go deeper when you want to

TIMEFEEDBACKDETUNEDRIFTDIFFUSIONTONEWIDTHMIXSTEREO PONG

Use GREEN as a focused one-knob effect or shape every part of its delay architecture.

Audio-reactive interface

GREEN’s trench-tunnel field moves with the sound. It accelerates with incoming audio and audible repeats, then rapidly falls back to a slow crawl as the signal disappears. The interface doesn’t simply decorate the effect — it reflects its energy.

Factory sounds

Start with clean spaces, dark dub echoes, moving stereo delays, diffused ambient tails and dedicated ping-pong designs.

BLUE 038_BLU · LIQUID CORE / MODULATION

Turn sound into motion

BLUE is a fluid modulation effect that moves between subtle stereo shimmer and deep, turbulent transformation. Its single macro control coordinates width, phase, delay, feedback and evolving irregularity — creating movement that feels alive rather than looped.

One control. An entire body of water.

At low settings, BLUE adds gentle depth and spatial motion. Turn it further and the sound begins to bend, ripple and spread. At its highest settings, BLUE becomes wide, aggressive and beautifully unstable — while remaining musical and controlled.

Three water modes

CLEARBright, focused movement with crisp phase detail.
DEEPWide, fluid modulation with a balanced liquid character.
ABYSSDark, slow, immersive motion with powerful delay-driven depth.

A living interface

BROWN 039_BRN · BILATERAL TRANSIENT

Shape the hit. Hold the weight.

BROWN is a one-knob transient shaper built for fast, musical control. Turn left to soften attacks and reduce sustain. Turn right to sharpen transients and bring the body forward. Return to the centre for zero processing. No hunting through parameters. No interruption to the session. Just immediate control over how a sound strikes, breathes and sits in the mix.

One knob. Two directions.

REDUCE −Tame hard attacks, shorten sustain and push sounds deeper into the mix.
CENTRE 0Transparent neutral position with no transient shaping.
RAISE +Add definition, increase sustain and give drums, bass, percussion and recorded material more presence.

Simple on the surface. Serious underneath.

Behind BROWN’s single performance control is the transient engine from HORME, combining attack and sustain shaping into one carefully balanced movement. For detailed work, open the Advanced panel to access the complete multiband transient system.

Built to move

BROWN’s rectilinear data weave responds to the incoming audio. Lines extend, structures flicker and windows open as the signal drives the plug-in — then settle into a quiet, static texture at silence. The display follows the sound, not the clock.

GREY 040_GRY · COMPRESSOR

Compression, without the complication

GREY is a one-knob compressor designed to control peaks, strengthen dynamics and keep your output level consistent — without interrupting your creative flow.

Turn the central control to move from gentle levelling to firm, confident compression. Behind the simplicity, GREY carefully coordinates threshold, ratio, knee, attack, hold and release. Its always-on automatic makeup gain restores the body of the signal while preserving the reduced peaks.

The display reads the music

The animated pin matrix responds directly to the music: quiet signals form a subtle suspended skyline, while stronger compression fills the surface with movement and amber gain-reduction flashes.

One knob. Complete dynamics control.

GREY gives you the control of a full compressor with the immediacy of a single decision.

BLACK 041_BLK · LIMITER / CLIPPER

Limit. Clip. Control.

BLACK is a one-knob limiter and clipper built to make loudness feel immediate. Turn the central control to move seamlessly from controlled peak limiting into assertive clipping. Low settings tighten and contain. Push further for density, impact and unmistakable edge.

The circular labyrinth responds to the processing itself — expanding as BLACK works, thickening under pressure and flashing coral when peaks are caught.

One knob. A complete dynamics path.

The main control coordinates drive, limiting and clipping as one continuous gesture. It gives you a wide usable range without forcing you to manage a wall of parameters. Fast to set. Easy to trust. Capable of serious weight.
