HORME

MultiMode Transient Designer

RETURN_TO_LAB STORE

HORME is a six-band transient designer for macOS. You set the frequency range its onset detector listens to, independently of the signal it shapes, so you decide what counts as a hit without filtering what anyone hears. Zero latency, at every setting.

There is no oversampling. Parameter moves are smoothed, band solos crossfade, and the detector's phase gates ride continuous envelope differences rather than switching. For drum and percussion work, sound design, SFX editing, game audio, post, and foley.

HOW_IT_WORKS

Six Linkwitz-Riley 4th-order bands split at 120 Hz, 400 Hz, 1 kHz, 3 kHz and 8 kHz. The split is telescoping, built as a difference of low-passes, so the six bands sum back to the input exactly, at no latency cost.

Inside each band, three detectors run in the dB domain behind a period-scaled power smoother, which is what stops sustained tonal content from reading as one permanent onset:

  • · ATTACK reads the gap between a fast and a mid envelope, the onset itself.
  • · SUSTAIN reads how far the signal has fallen below its own recent peak. Both of its envelopes attack identically, so it is blind to the onset by construction and cannot fight ATTACK for the same moment.
  • · DECAY reads the late tail, gated past-peak and muted while the onset detector is still reading.

Positive is more of that region, negative is less. Every gate between the three is a continuous weight rather than a switch, so the gain law has no steps in it anywhere.

WHAT YOU SEE

SIMPLE is one large live envelope window: the input envelope, the shaped trace over it, and a peak readout. Four draggable nodes sit on a log-frequency overlay, two set the high-pass and low-pass corners the onset detector listens to, two are the transient-keyed bells. Drag for frequency, vertical for bell depth, command-drag for Q. The bell nodes pulse with the live onset key, so you can see the detector firing.

MULTIBAND is six per-band envelope panes with crossover labels, per-band ATTACK and SUSTAIN offsets summed onto the masters, band on/off, and LISTEN to solo one band's shaped slice. The SHAPE row, ATTACK, SUSTAIN, DECAY, MIX, OUTPUT: is shared by both pages.

DETECTION FOCUS

Two nodes set a high-pass and a low-pass on the detector, not the audio. Aim them at the stick and the shell stays out of the decision; aim them low and cymbal wash stops triggering the shaping. The signal itself passes unfiltered either way.

At its default corners the path is not merely inaudible, it is bypassed outright, and the per-band weights are exact multiplicative identity.

TRANSIENT-KEYED BELLS

Two peaking EQ bands whose gain is driven by the live onset key from the focused detector. They open on the hit and sit flat between hits, so nothing static rests on the signal. At depth 0 the section is skipped entirely rather than run at unity, the honest way to be bit-identical when unused.
