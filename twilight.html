Voices in orbit.

Five voices. Place them in a field, link them, set them moving, and their motion becomes the modulation that drives everything else.

Most synthesizers modulate with lanes, an LFO here, an envelope there, a matrix to wire them together. TWILIGHT modulates with physics and relationships.

Its five voices live in a field. You place each one: left to right is stereo position, near to far is presence. Link them and they swing together as one rigid constellation, orbiting under a gravity you control. That motion isn't decoration, any voice's position can be tethered to any parameter in the instrument, so the same gesture that carries a voice across the room also stirs a grain cloud, opens a reverb, or bends another voice's pitch.

The voices are relational too. One can follow another at an interval, and inverting that harmony gives you its octave complement rather than a sign flip. Patterns work the same way: a voice can borrow another's groove while keeping its own pitch, then run it backwards, or mirrored.

Play it and it's an instrument. Leave it and it generates.

HOW_IT_WORKS

C O N S T E L L A T I O N // VOICES AS BODIES

Five voices in a pan-and-depth field. Far voices sit back, quieter and darker; near ones are present and bright. ORBIT swings the linked ones as a single rigid shape. GRAVITY draws the constellation inward or flings it wide. HEAT drives voices harder as they crowd the centre. And GRAVITY PULSE locks the whole field to your host tempo, 1/1 down to 1/16, so the image collapses and blooms on the beat.

T E T H E R S // MOTION AS MODULATION

Right-click any knob to tether it: to a voice's position, to gravity itself, or to the note you just played, pitch, velocity, a fresh random per keystroke. Eight routes, any eligible parameter, bipolar depth you can drag straight from the knob's ring. Because the sources are physical, a modulation shape is something you place and watch, not a curve you draw.

R E L A T I O N A L V O I C E S

Voices harmonize against each other rather than in isolation, and it holds in both drone and keyboard modes, change the root and the whole structure follows. Chord presets set the relationships in one move.

T H E E N G I N E S

DRIFT doubles and detunes into a slowly shifting harmonic field. FIELD is a reverb with a grain engine, smear the tail into a cloud, or stutter it into rhythm. FADE breathes. The OUTPUT MATRIX finishes: ring modulation with selectable oversampling, flanging, stutter, and a lookahead limiter.

P L A Y I T

Sixteen-voice polyphony, per-voice envelopes, velocity, your keyboard. Or leave it in drone mode and let it run, the ambience it was born doing is a preset away.

RECORDING_AND_CAPTURE

A built-in REC function captures the instrument's output straight to a WAV file, so a long drone can be printed without leaving the plug-in.

FOUR_PALETTES

DUSK, EMBER, GROVE and TERRA. Pick one from the header; it follows you across sessions.
