The idea

Most synthesizers modulate with lanes: an LFO here, an envelope there, a matrix to wire them together. TWILIGHT modulates with physics and relationships.

Its five voices live in a field. Left to right is stereo position, near to far is presence. Link them and they swing together as one rigid constellation, orbiting under a gravity you control.

That motion is not decoration. Any voice's position can be tethered to any parameter, so the gesture that carries a voice across the room also stirs a grain cloud, opens a reverb or bends another voice's pitch.

The voices are relational too. One can follow another at an interval, and a voice can borrow another's groove while keeping its own pitch, then run it backwards or mirrored.

Constellation
  • THE FIELDFive voices in a pan-and-depth field: four oscillators and one you load a sample into. Far voices sit back, quieter and darker; near ones are present and bright.
  • ORBITSwings the linked voices as a single rigid shape.
  • GRAVITYDraws the constellation inward or flings it wide.
  • HEATDrives voices harder as they crowd the centre.
  • GRAVITY PULSELocks the whole field to your host tempo, from 1/1 down to 1/16, so the image collapses and blooms on the beat.
Tethers

Right-click any knob to tether it: to a voice's position, to gravity itself, or to the note you just played, by pitch, velocity or a fresh random value per keystroke.

Eight routes, any eligible parameter, with a bipolar depth you drag straight from the knob's ring. Because the sources are physical, a modulation shape is something you place and watch, not a curve you draw.

Relational voices. Voices harmonize against each other rather than in isolation, in drone and keyboard modes alike. Change the root and the whole structure follows; inverting a harmony gives its octave complement. Chord presets set the relationships in one move.

Each voice has its own wave, frequency, beat, level and pattern, so the four oscillators can beat against one another as binaural pairs.

The engines
  1. 1THETA

    Four oscillator voices and a sample voice.

  2. 2CONSTELLATION

    Place, link, orbit, gravity and heat.

  3. 3DRIFT

    Two layers that double and detune into a slowly shifting field.

  4. 4FIELD

    A shimmer reverb with a grain engine: smear or stutter.

  5. 5FADE

    A breathing gate, from hard pulse to sidechain duck.

  6. 6OUTPUT MATRIX

    Ring mod, flanger, stutter and a lookahead limiter.

The ring modulator runs with selectable oversampling, up to 8x, and every stage has its own switch, so TWILIGHT can be one sine voice or the whole machine.

Play it

Sixteen-voice polyphony, per-voice envelopes, velocity and your keyboard, in mono or poly. Or leave it in drone mode and let it run: the ambience TWILIGHT was born making is a preset away.

Four palettes, DUSK, EMBER, GROVE and TERRA, sit in the header. Pick one and it follows you across sessions.
