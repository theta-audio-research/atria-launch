Primary Chassis // Host Shell

Your plugins. Contorted.

CONTORTA wraps around any VST3 or AU and gives it everything your DAW never could — granular randomization, living drift, and modulation that reaches every parameter. While your automation lanes keep working.

THE_PROBLEM

Most plugins don't have a randomize button. The ones that do give you a grenade: one click, every knob, no survivors. And unless you're willing to switch DAWs, nothing on earth lets you modulate any parameter of any plugin.

CONTORTA fixes all three. It's not an effect. It's a host with bad intentions.

DEVELOPMENT_STATUS

Host shell, 64 proxy lanes, two-way sync, gesture relay and the automatable trigger are running. The modulation rack and drift engine are in build — see the roadmap below.

RUNNING_NOW

HOST ANYTHING

Drop any VST3 or AU inside CONTORTA — synth, compressor, that weird freeware flanger from 2009. Its parameters become CONTORTA's parameters. All of them, up for grabs. Audio and MIDI pass straight through the hosted plugin, its latency is forwarded to the DAW, and CONTORTA adds nothing of its own.

RANDOMIZE LIKE YOU MEAN IT

This is not a randomize button. It's a randomize system. Lock what's sacred, dial in probability and amount, and roll the dice on exactly the chaos you ordered — and nothing you didn't.

  • · Per-parameter locks — hold the knobs that matter exactly where you put them.
  • · Probability and amount — from a subtle re-seasoning to a full teardown.
  • · Per-knob ranges — let the filter roam, keep the output gain on a leash. Engine-side today; the range editor lands with the UI pass.
A DICE ROLL YOU CAN SEQUENCE

CONTORTA's randomize trigger is itself an automatable parameter. Draw it into your arrangement. Fire it every bar, every drop, every time the vocal stops. Your patch becomes a living thing that re-invents itself on the grid.

AUTOMATION THAT SURVIVES

The dirty secret of every plugin host: once your plugin lives inside it, your DAW's automation goes blind. CONTORTA's proxy lanes stay fully automatable and recordable — and when you grab a knob on the hosted plugin's own UI, CONTORTA relays the touch so your DAW writes the automation. The lanes are stable and permanent: swap the hosted plugin and your lanes stay put. Nothing about your workflow breaks. It just bends.
