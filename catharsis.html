CATHARSIS

Dual-Engine Noise Reduction

RETURN_TO_LAB STORE

Katharsis — the Greek word for cleansing. Aristotle used it for what tragedy does to an audience: the purging that leaves only what matters. It is the right name for a tool whose whole job is to take away — the air conditioning, the room hum, the camera whine, the hiss under a lavalier — and leave the performance standing clean.

CATHARSIS is the noise-reduction engine lifted out of ROGERIAN, THETA's dialogue-processing suite, and offered as a focused, single-purpose plug-in. The same denoiser that clears a location recording inside ROGERIAN's full chain now installs on its own. It was voiced for dialogue, but it works on anything with a noise floor: a hissy guitar amp, a noisy synth, an archive transfer, a podcast recorded in a spare room.

TWO_ENGINES // ONE_SET_OF_CONTROLS

Most denoisers make you live with their single compromise. CATHARSIS gives you both sides of it and lets you switch with one click.

  • · MULTIBAND — genuinely zero latency. Six fixed bands, each running a cut-only downward expander against a continuously measured noise floor. The band split is built from cascaded Linkwitz-Riley crossovers arranged so the six bands sum back to exactly the input — perfect reconstruction, no phase smear, and not one sample of delay. Safe to track through, safe to monitor through, and safe against picture.
  • · SPECTRAL — the deep clean. An MMSE log-spectral-amplitude estimator with decision-directed SNR: the classical approach specifically designed to avoid the warbling, sparkling “musical noise” that cheap spectral subtraction leaves behind. It reads the recording as a moving picture of frequency against time and lifts the noise out from underneath the voice. Roughly 11 ms of latency, fully reported to your host.

Switching engines is handled cleanly — a short fade covers the changeover so the latency change can never click, and the host is re-notified so plug-in delay compensation stays correct. The footer always shows which engine is running and exactly what it costs you.

IT FINDS THE NOISE FOR YOU
  • · AUTO tracks the noise floor continuously, following it as the room changes across a take. Nothing to set up — drop it on and it adapts.
  • · LEARN captures a profile from a passage of noise-only audio and freezes it. Find the room tone between lines, hit LEARN NOISE, and the expander works against that fingerprint instead of a live estimate — the right choice when the noise is consistent and you want the cleaning to be too.
  • · SUGGEST reads what it has measured and sets a sensible starting point for you, biasing the bands that are actually noisy a little harder than the ones that aren't.
SHAPE IT BY HAND

In MULTIBAND, six numbered handles sit across the frequency display:

  • · Drag a handle up for more reduction in that band — pull hiss out of the top without touching the chest of a voice, or clear a rumble without thinning the whole recording.
  • · Double-click to bypass a band entirely.
  • · Shift-click to LISTEN — audition only what that band is removing. This is the honest way to set a denoiser: if you can hear voice in the removal, you have gone too far, and you will know immediately instead of three hours later.

Alongside them: AMOUNT for how much comes out, SENS for how far above the floor the expander starts working, ATTACK and RELEASE for how quickly it moves, and MIX to blend the cleaned signal back against the original when total removal sounds less natural than partial removal.

SEE EXACTLY WHAT YOU'RE TAKING AWAY

The display puts zero on the centre line and splits the job in two. Above it, the live input spectrum — and, in SPECTRAL, the noise floor the engine has learned, drawn as its own line so you can see what it believes is noise. Below it, in a deep violet heat that brightens as it deepens, exactly what is being removed, right now, across the whole spectrum.

Most denoisers show you a spectrum and leave you guessing. This one shows you the subtraction.

TRANSPARENT UNTIL YOU ASK

CATHARSIS opens with AMOUNT at zero, and at zero it is mathematically transparent — the multiband engine's output is bit-identical to its input, sample for sample. There is no separate on switch to forget about and no “off” that still colours the signal. Turn AMOUNT up and the plug-in starts working; leave it down and it is not there. BYPASS is a click-free crossfade against a properly delay-aligned dry signal, so A/B comparisons are honest in both engines.
