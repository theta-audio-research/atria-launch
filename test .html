<div class="stripes-thin"></div>

<p>GREEN is a one-knob delay and echo built around detuned, drifting tails. Set the tempo by tapping the main control, then turn it to move from a restrained echo to a wide, diffused field of shifting repeats.</p>

<p><strong>Your delay time stays locked. The knob controls the character and intensity around it.</strong></p>


<h3>One knob. A whole ecosystem of echoes.</h3>

<p>Turn GREEN to increase the wet level, feedback, detune, drift, diffusion, width, and tonal depth together. Every part of the sound evolves along one carefully tuned musical curve.</p>

<p>At lower settings, repeats remain clean and controlled. Push further and they spread, darken, soften, and move increasingly out of tune.</p>


<h3>Tap. Turn. Let it grow.</h3>

<p>Tap the main knob to set the tempo. GREEN detects and averages your rhythm, then locks the delay to a quarter-note pulse. Changing the knob afterward won’t disturb the timing.</p>


<h3>Detuned tails</h3>

<p>Every pass through the feedback path accumulates subtle opposing pitch movement. The center stays grounded while the tail becomes wider, less stable, and more alive.</p>


<h3>Stereo Pong</h3>

<p>Activate Stereo Pong to send the repeats alternating across the stereo field. Each bounce continues through GREEN’s detuning, diffusion, tone, and drift architecture.</p>


<h3>Go deeper when you want to</h3>

<p>Open the Advanced panel for direct access to:</p>

<ul>

    <li>Time</li>

    <li>Feedback</li>

    <li>Detune</li>

    <li>Drift</li>

    <li>Diffusion</li>

    <li>Mix</li>

    <li>Stereo Pong</li>

</ul>

<p>Use GREEN as a focused one-knob effect or shape every part of its delay architecture.</p>


<h3>Audio-reactive interface</h3>

<p>GREEN’s trench-tunnel field moves with the sound. It accelerates with incoming audio and audible repeats, then rapidly falls back to a slow crawl as the signal disappears. The interface doesn’t simply decorate the effect—it reflects its energy.</p>
