THE_IDEA

CREOSOTE is not a picture of a console. It is a console, photographed: panels, caps and instruments rendered in Blender Cycles under one locked studio light, with real bulb-backlit VUs, chunky bronze selectors, and light that spills organically across the plate.

Behind the glass it is a measured record chain. The tape machine is calibrated so 0 VU sits at −18 dBFS. The compressors are eight separate topologies rather than eight presets. The summing bus runs anti-derivative anti-aliasing throughout. Marketing says warm; we publish the figures.

Signal path: GRIT (tape) → AMBER (tubes) → COMPRESS → SUMMING → OUTPUT EQ → STAGING (level, tone, limit), with the AGE LFO driving the tape transport’s wow and flutter.

THE_CHAIN

GRIT: the tape machine

Seven transports. Ten reels. Three speeds. One WEAR knob that ages the whole transport at once.

  • · 7 machines: A800, ATR-102, JH-24, MTR-90, MM1200, 280 and a prosumer reel.
  • · 10 tape formulas: 456, 406/407, 499, GP9, 250, 996, SM468, SM911, SM900 and a master-grade reel.
  • · Four mini-effects: PUMP, SPEED, DRIFT and HEADWEAR. Double-click to engage, drag for amount, option-drag for mix.
  • · Calibration: ¼" to 2" 24-track, 7.5/15/30 IPS, +3/+6/+9 cal, over and under bias.
  • · Calibrated saturator: 0 VU = −18 dBFS, 0.39% THD at operating level.
  • · Program-dependent hysteresis: the tape warms up and remembers soft attacks and lingering tails.
  • · Record-amp slew limiting: the HF transient rounding of a real record amplifier.
  • · Comb-free MIX: the dry blend leg runs through a matched oversampling path.
AMBER: the tube stage

A three-stage valve amplifier you can re-tube, re-bias and re-era.

  • · Preamp: 12AX7, 12AT7, 12AU7, EF86.
  • · Power: EL84, EL34, 6L6, 6V6, KT66, KT88.
  • · Rectifier: 5Y3, GZ34, with era-true sag.
  • · Per-stage voicings: condition, bias point (cold / normal / hot), plate voltage and load.
  • · Hardware modes: direct, transformer-coupled and passive-EQ output paths.
  • · Calibrated drive law: the whole knob is useful, with RMS auto-makeup holding your level steady.
COMPRESS: eight compressors, true circuits

Not eight presets of one compressor. Eight true hardware topologies, each with its own detector behaviour.

  • · The models: LEVEL CELL (opto), BLACK FET (1176), ALL BUTTONS, TUBE MU (vari-mu), VCA BUS, DIODE BRIDGE, PWM, ZENER.
  • · True topology: opto, vari-mu, bridge and PWM units run feedback; FET, VCA and Zener units run feedforward. Force either topology in TWEAK.
  • · Infrastructure: always-on lookahead with full delay compensation, sidechain filtering, and a real edgewise gain-reduction meter.
SUMMING, EQ & STAGING: the console bus

Character saturation, stereo glue and a ceiling clipper, all anti-derivative anti-aliased, all measured.

  • · Summing: PREGAIN, WIDTH (M/S), BALANCE. The last analog bus before the wide world.
  • · IRON: a modeled output transformer (off / subtle / rich) with three voicings, British console, American punch, broadcast desk, built as a flux integrator with magnetic remanence rather than an EQ curve.
  • · Staging: honest INPUT/LEVEL staging, a limit lookahead ceiling pouring oil-damped reduction top-down, and AUTO LEVEL for one-button unity out.
  • · TONE: a direction-aware tilt. Darkening reaches into the presence region to warm rather than dull; brightening lifts the air band and tightens lows.
OUTPUT EQ: the chart plate

Eight bands of dynamic EQ drawn on a machine-tool chart: cream card, blue graticule, red pen.

  • · Dynamic architecture: per-band frequency, gain and Q, plus dynamic gain with attack and release. Each band can duck or expand itself against the signal.
  • · Visual feedback: pre and post curves displayed together, the dashed blueprint and the live red line, with the dynamic delta filled between them.
  • · Decramped, analog-matched filters: shelves and bells matched to their analog prototypes to within 0.16 dB at 20 kHz. The air band behaves like the circuit, not the sample rate.
TRANSPORT & LFO: phosphor logic

A real transport with scrape-band noise, head-gap width and dropout aging, all driven by WEAR.

  • · The oscilloscope: wow and flutter with per-channel phase offset, authored on the AGE LFO page.
  • · Shape vectors: five shapes including sample-and-hold, with rate, depth, smoothing and phase.
  • · Hardware CRT: drawn live on a green phosphor CRT with an amber etched graticule.

SIGNATURE_FEATURES

Unit Variance

Real hardware never matched. Pick UNIT 1 through 16 and the instance adopts that unit's component tolerances: filter corners drift up to ±5%, drive points shift fractions of a dB, the channels mismatch slightly. Deterministic, recall-safe, loudness-matched to ±0.005 dB.

Punch Protection

Heavy drive usually costs you your transients. PUNCH detects onsets and momentarily eases the drive into the saturators, level-compensated and wet-path only, so the crack of the stick passes clean while the body stays thick. Measured: attacks pass around 1.4 dB cleaner at FULL.

Session Safety

Every audible improvement ships behind a DSP version gate. A session saved on any previous version recalls bit-identical while new sessions get the newest behaviour, enforced with an automated 41-bar audio regression suite on every build.

Measured, Not Marketed

0.39% THD at 0 VU. Alias floors driven down 16 to 35 dB by ADAA. EQ matched to analog prototypes within 0.16 dB at 20 kHz. Comb-free wet/dry blending verified by null test.

Era-Anchored Presets

Seventeen starting points voiced from classic recording chains of each era, plus the THETA artist set. All loudness-matched to unity.

Photographed UI

Panels, caps and instruments rendered in Blender Cycles under one locked studio light. Real bulb-backlit VUs, chunky bronze selectors, and light that spills organically across the plate.
