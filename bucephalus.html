BUCEPHALUS

BUCEPHALUS is a stereo delay with independent left and right times, three time effects in front of it, nine tail effects inside its feedback loop, and five pages of tools for ducking, motion, rhythm, character and diffusion.

It is built to one rule: only clean sources go round the loop. Repeats can be stuttered, shuffled, frozen, driven and dirtied, but nothing that smears is allowed into the core, so the tail never turns to mud.

A clean delay core

Most delays let anything into the loop, and every pass through a reverb-like stage blurs the next one. A few repeats in, the tail is a wash. BUCEPHALUS only feeds its core with clean sources: the dry signal, the repeats themselves, stutters, shuffled slices and frozen loops.

Those sources can be pushed hard. They can be reversed, grained, gated, filtered, crushed into tape or bucket-brigade colour, and driven. But anything that smears sits after the repeats, on what you hear, never on what goes back round.

The rule is tested, not just intended. A single click is sent through every input effect at extreme settings and has to come out as a handful of clean clicks. A diffused wash is run through the same test as the control, and it has to fail.

That is why Diffusion lives on the listening return. It scatters the echoes you hear, and the loop carries on repeating the clean ones.

Signal path
  1. 1INPUT

    Three time slots in series before the delay: Gated stutter, Slice shuffle or Freeze / recapture, in any order.

  2. 2DELAY CORE

    Independent left and right times, 50 ms to 4 s, free, tapped or synced to the host. Feedback and Character work here.

  3. 3TAIL

    Nine effects inside the feedback loop, so every repeat is treated again on its way round.

  4. 4RETURN

    Ducking, Diffusion, Tone and Air shape what you hear without touching what is stored in the loop.

  5. 5OUTPUT

    Dry, Return and Master levels, then a smooth peak ceiling just under full scale.

Input time slots

Three slots run one after another in front of the delay. Each can hold any of the three time effects, including the same one twice, and each has its own Amount, where zero is off. Click 1, 2 or 3 to open that slot's controls. A slot keeps its settings when you change its type, so you can try another and come back.

Slots sync to the delay's clock, from 1/64 to 1/2, or run free in milliseconds. CAPTURE takes a new stutter or freeze on demand. The dry signal is never delayed by any of it.

  • GATED STUTTERAudio crossing the trigger is captured, then played back as a burst of repeats. Set the slice, the repeat rate, how many repeats, the pulse width, and Accelerate to tighten the burst up to four times as it goes.
  • SLICE SHUFFLERecords phrases and plays the last one back in a new order while it records the next. Two to sixteen slices, a shuffle amount, and reversal of individual slices.
  • FREEZE / RECAPTUREHolds a stereo loop after the source stops. Recapture on demand or every one to eight loop lengths, with Overdub to blend the held loop into the next one.
The tail, inside the loop

The Tail card has nine knobs, and every one of them is its own effect. There are no mode menus and no enable buttons: turn a knob up from zero and it is in, and they all run together.

Because the tail sits inside the feedback loop, a repeat goes through it again on every pass. A little Grain becomes more grain with each echo; a High-pass thins the tail as it fades; Width pushes the repeats further out each time round, with gain compensation so the loop stays under control even at 92% feedback.

  • REVERSE · GRAINEach with its own amount and history. Zero bypasses the stage.
  • PAN · WIDTH · CROSSFEEDIndependent. Width runs from mono through normal to double side width.
  • TREMOLOA full-depth sine, 0 to 12 Hz.
  • GATEA real threshold gate, about −72 dB to 0 dB, stereo-linked, with no hard switching.
  • HIGH-PASS · LOW-PASSSeparate 12 dB per octave filters that can work together.
Core tools

Five tabs sit beside MAIN. Each opens a page of tools under the echo walls, which keep running while you work. Every page has its own ON switch.

  • DUCKINGTurns the repeats down while you play and lets them back up when you stop. Amount up to 30 dB, Recovery from 20 ms to 2 s. It acts on what you hear, so the stored repeats are never erased.
  • MOTIONTwo LFOs (sine, triangle or smooth drift, free or synced) and an envelope follower, sent to delay time, tail pan, width, low-pass, each input slot's Amount, or return level. A shared stereo phase spreads them across left and right.
  • RHYTHMAn alternating rhythm engine: straight, dotted and triplet divisions from 1/64 to a bar, a separate first echo, swing up to 45%, and Alternate reduction to drop every second echo.
  • CHARACTERClean, Tape, BBD and Early digital voicings, with input and feedback drive up to 24 dB. Bias, Sag and Rounding shape the loop; Tone and Air colour the return only. An HQ mode adds more oversampling.
  • DIFFUSIONA four-stage stereo all-pass network that scatters the echoes on the listening return, with Amount and Size. It never feeds back into the core.
Character

The four voicings are original, not emulations of particular units. Tape rounds the peaks and limits the bandwidth. BBD gets darker as the repeat time gets longer, with a little grit. Early digital brings 13-bit-style grain and a narrow top end.

Input drive saturates what goes into the delay. Feedback drive saturates only what comes back round, so the first echo is never touched by it and each pass after that gets a little warmer.

Every drive stage runs oversampled with anti-aliasing, so pushing it hard adds harmonics, not the harsh, inharmonic fizz of aliasing. At full drive the alias products in the audible band sit more than 60 dB under the note.

With Clean selected and no drive, the delay is bit-for-bit a plain delay, and the plug-in adds no latency.

Echo walls

The display in the middle of the panel shows what the delay is actually doing. Wet audio enters at the centre and travels outward, left repeats to the left and right repeats to the right. Each repeat bends the walls by how loud it is, and by where its energy sits: bass bends the lower lines, treble the upper ones.

Nothing in it is invented. Every line, the glow behind the glass and the turning Feedback corona come from the measured wet signal. When the repeats stop, the walls go flat.

The Listen card switches between the stereo output, Input delta and Tail delta. A delta plays only what that bank adds or takes away, so you can hear exactly what the stutter, the gate or the filters are doing. Each bank also has its own Bypass.

A / B holds two complete sounds to switch between. Mix lock keeps your Dry / wet and levels where they are while you browse presets.

PRODUCERS

Put synced, independent left and right repeats on a vocal or a lead, duck them under the phrase, and let them bloom in the gaps.

SOUND DESIGNERS

Freeze a moment, shuffle it, stutter it, and send it round a loop that degrades it into tape or bucket-brigade colour without ever turning to mush.

MIX ENGINEERS

A delay you can leave on a return: clean at Clean, measured gain staging, a peak ceiling, and Delta buttons to hear exactly what each bank adds.

DUB & AMBIENT

Push feedback, drive the loop and ride the tail filters. Repeats darken and thicken pass by pass, and Clear tails is one click away.
